01 / inspiration

It started in a room
we couldn't hear.

At a campus research fair, one of us could not make out the speaker. He turned to the person beside him and still could not hear. The room was full of voices.

That moment sparked One Voice. We wanted a simple choice: see someone, choose them, hear them.

One person. One conversation. More connection.
02 / how we built it

Tethered today.
Worn tomorrow.

Our printed frame holds an intact Logitech C270 camera. The laptop runs live voice isolation when a supported GPU is available. Wired earphones play the result. We are still checking how the frame fits different faces.

The C270 captures faces and room audio. The browser lets you choose a person. The laptop runs the voice model. Captions and notes are optional.

03 / what it does

Sight tells sound
who to follow.

Pick a face on screen. On a GPU laptop, One Voice lifts that person's voice above the room. The camera keeps track of whom you chose. Optional lip cues can help when enabled.

The About page tells the story. The listening app opens the camera and microphone when you start a live session. Hear the result through wired earphones.

04 / features

Listening is
where it starts.

See what works now. Then peek at what comes next.

Live isolation

Live GPU demo. Pick a visible person. Hear their voice rise above the room.

Live captions

Off by default. Turn on captions to read the chosen person's words. Selected audio goes to ElevenLabs while captions are on.

Alarm awareness

Experimental. If the optional detector is active and spots an alarm, it can bring back room audio. Do not rely on it for safety.

Conversation notes

On demand. Turn on captions first. Press Summarize to send the text to Gemini and get short notes.

Visual context

Future idea. Choose a snapshot and ask what is in view. This feature is not in the listening app yet.

05 / our journey

Built with care.
Still getting better.

Challenges we hit

Our CPU laptop cannot run live isolation. We made a preview so we could test the interface. We still need more fit tests.

What we are proud of

We printed a frame and built a way to pick a face. On a GPU laptop, that choice drives the live audio path.

What we learned

A clear choice matters. So do clear states. We made the preview obvious and kept captions off until you ask for them.

What is next

Test the frame on more faces. Measure the sound before and after isolation. Explore a smaller camera and more local processing.

06 / try it
